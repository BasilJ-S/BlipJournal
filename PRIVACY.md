# Blip Journal privacy policy

Last updated: November 10, 2026

Blip Journal does not collect any data.

- **No network.** The app makes no network requests. It has no account, no sign-up, no
  server, no analytics and no advertising. Nothing you enter is sent to the developer or
  to anyone else.
- **Your data stays on your device.** Surveys, answers and settings are stored only on
  your iPhone, encrypted at rest by iOS Data Protection, and the app opens with Face ID
  or your passcode.
- **Prompts are local.** Prompts are local notifications scheduled on your iPhone. No
  push service is involved.
- **Exports and backups you share.** When you export CSV, a JSON backup or a survey
  file, the file is plain and unencrypted, so anyone who has it can read it. It goes
  only where you choose to send or save it. A survey file holds only the questions, not
  your answers.
- **Device backups.** Like other app data, your Blip Journal data is included in iCloud
  or computer backups of your iPhone if you have them turned on. Those backups are
  handled by Apple and your settings, not by Blip Journal.
- **Deleting your data.** You can delete entries or wipe everything from Settings.
  Deleting the app removes its data from your iPhone.

The app is open source under the MIT licence, so you can check these statements in the
code: https://github.com/BasilJ-S/BlipJournal

Questions: https://github.com/BasilJ-S/BlipJournal/issues
